The thirty-second version

Managed democracy, unmanaged laughter.

The only game where 'friendly fire' is a personality trait.

HELLDIVERS 2 is the funniest co-op shooter any of us have played since Left 4 Dead. Missions are the perfect thirty to forty minutes, the stratagems are stupid in the best way, and the difference between 'we succeeded' and 'we killed each other' is one badly-thrown grenade. We'd immediately re-buy it on any platform we owned.

Okay, what is this thing?

One of us threw an orbital laser to solve a bug nest and killed all three of us in the same move. We laughed for a full minute. The mission failed. We started another one. That's the pitch. HELLDIVERS 2 is a game about calling in enormous ordnance and mostly hitting yourself, and it's built precisely for people who cannot take themselves seriously.

Under the physics comedy is a really good third-person shooter. Guns feel weighty, the stratagems open up a real toolkit of orbitals and support weapons and turrets, and difficulty scales so hard that Helldive missions become full-on tactical operations. The galactic war meta gives you a reason to log in, without ever guilt-tripping you when you don't. That last bit is the one we care about.

Why it escaped the group chat

  • Four-player co-op with proximity chat, dropped in via literal escape pod.
  • Stratagems are called down with directional inputs and they will kill you if you type wrong.
  • The war state changes based on what all players collectively do that week.

Three dads enter

01

Dad Who Skipped the Tutorial

I learnt to run and shout, and that's most of the game.

I pick a machine gun, point it away from the other two on request, and miss that request approximately half the time. Somehow I'm now the group's best flag-defender because I refuse to move and things die around me. I have not once opened the loadout screen and I keep getting promoted anyway. The universe rewards commitment.

02

The Descent Guy

Optimising the mid-mission call-in order. Absolutely my lane.

I have a spreadsheet. Of course I do. The mid-mission economy of when to burn a resupply, when to hold your Eagle strafing runs, and how to sequence stratagems around a heavy nest is exactly my kind of maths. I'm quietly convinced this is the first co-op shooter since 1999 that respects positioning, and honestly, the other two agree with me.

03

Normal-ish Dad

Missions end when they say they'll end. Blessed.

Forty-minute cap, hard extract, done. That's the whole reason it's stuck in our rotation. I can start a Helldive at nine and be in bed by ten, which is the correct shape of a Friday. My only asterisk: bring headphones because the proximity chat is half the joke, and don't play it solo, because it becomes a completely different, worse game.

Who it is for and what to watch

Invite these people

  • Groups who laugh when they die
  • Anyone whose ideal shooter is 'John Wick, dumb friends'
  • People who want a live service that doesn't need daily maintenance

Dad caveats

  • Solo play is technically supported and emotionally punishing
  • Server issues can flare, though they've calmed since launch
  • You will start yelling stratagem codes in your sleep
